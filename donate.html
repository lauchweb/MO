Help keep what is ours alive.

Mauritius has plants that exist nowhere else on Earth. Supporting NOU ANDEMIK helps us keep these species growing, make them better known and create the conditions for their continued presence beyond the places where they survive today.

What your support makes possible

Living collections

Propagating plants creates living populations that can be maintained, studied and grown beyond their original locations.

Knowledge

Better awareness helps people recognise the plants around them and understand why Mauritius’ endemic species are worth keeping.

Continuity

Long-term work requires time, care and consistency. Contributions help sustain the practical work behind that continuity.

The value of an endemic plant is not only that it is rare. It is that it belongs to this place. Keeping it alive means keeping a part of Mauritius alive with it.
