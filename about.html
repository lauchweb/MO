About NOU ANDEMIK

Keeping Mauritius’ endemic life present.

NOU ANDEMIK exists to give Mauritius’ native and endemic flora a stronger presence in the places where people live, learn and encounter the natural world. We work around the simple idea that plants cannot be protected only as names in books or specimens in collections. They need to remain alive, growing and understood.

Who We Are

NOU ANDEMIK is centred on the living plant heritage of Mauritius. Our work focuses on species that are part of the island’s natural identity, particularly plants that occur nowhere else in the world. We bring together propagation, practical conservation and public appreciation to help give these species a continuing place in Mauritius.

Why We Exist

Mauritius has a botanical heritage shaped by millions of years of evolution and by the particular landscapes of an island. Some of its plants are found only here. Their disappearance would therefore mean more than the loss of individual plants: it would remove something that cannot be recreated somewhere else.

We believe that keeping these species present requires more than concern for their survival. It requires living material, people who know these plants, places where they can be grown, and a culture that recognises their value.

What We Do

Our nursery is one practical part of this work. Through propagation, plants can be grown beyond the limits of their remaining natural populations and maintained as living material. This creates opportunities for continued cultivation, education and future conservation efforts.

We also work to make endemic flora more visible and familiar. Understanding what grows naturally on the island is an important part of valuing it. By sharing knowledge and making these plants more accessible, we hope to encourage greater care for the landscapes and species that make Mauritius distinct.

Living Heritage

A plant that is still growing can still be known, studied, cultivated and protected. For NOU ANDEMIK, conservation begins with keeping that possibility alive.

Our Direction

Our aim is not to treat endemic flora as something that belongs only to the past. We want these plants to have a future in which they are recognised as part of everyday Mauritius: present in cultivation, understood by the public and considered in the way the island cares for its natural spaces.

The work is necessarily long-term. Populations take time to establish, knowledge takes time to spread, and meaningful conservation depends on continuity. NOU ANDEMIK is built around that continuity — keeping plants growing while helping keep their place in the story of Mauritius.
