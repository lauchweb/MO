About us

Committed to our native heritage.

Our work focuses on safeguarding the species that make Mauritius botanically unique and ensuring their place in the landscapes of future generations.

Our commitment

We are committed to the conservation of the species that are part of Mauritius’ natural heritage, focusing on its indigenous and endemic flora.

Our purpose

We seek to support the long-term preservation of Mauritius’ botanical heritage and encourage greater appreciation of the plants that form an important part of the island’s natural identity.
