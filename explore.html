Our Flora

Mauritius is home to a remarkable diversity of plant life. Its forests, wetlands, mountains and coastal areas support plants that have adapted to the island’s different environments over generations.

Native & Endemic

Native plants occur naturally in Mauritius, while endemic plants are found naturally only in Mauritius and nowhere else. These species form an important part of the island’s natural heritage and deserve particular care and protection.

Discover the Species

Explore the plants that make up our botanical heritage and learn about their characteristics, habitats and importance. Each species tells part of the story of Mauritius and its natural environment.

Why They Matter

Plants support the ecosystems around them, provide food and shelter for wildlife, and contribute to the health of our natural landscapes. Protecting them means protecting the living environment that connects them all.
