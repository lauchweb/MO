Propagation · Presence · Continuity

Keeping what is ours alive.

Our nursery propagates Mauritius’ native and endemic plants, helping keep the island’s unique botanical heritage present, protected and growing.

Propagation is part of conservation. By growing endemic plants, we help maintain living populations of the flora that belongs uniquely to Mauritius.
